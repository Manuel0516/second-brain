# Sweden and Spain scope

This file defines product scope, not personal tax advice.

## Sweden profile capabilities

- Residency/unlimited-vs-limited liability evidence workspace.
- Foreign-income inventory.
- Employment/university income schedule.
- Funds, shares and dividends schedule.
- Crypto disposal and staking event inventory.
- Foreign withholding records.
- SEK valuation policy.
- Supporting evidence and open questions.

## Spain profile capabilities

- Full-year resident/non-resident status workspace.
- Foreign income and account inventory.
- Funds, securities and gold schedules.
- Crypto disposal and reward inventory.
- Foreign-asset reporting candidate inventory, including separate handling for virtual currencies.
- EUR valuation policy.
- Supporting evidence and open questions.

## Double-residency UX

The module should show three separate layers:

1. **Presence timeline** — observed days and evidence.
2. **Domestic criteria checklist** — candidate status in each jurisdiction.
3. **Treaty-review workspace** — unresolved tie-breaker facts and adviser conclusion.

Do not collapse these into one “country” field.

## Important implementation implication

Rules and thresholds can change. All user-facing conclusions must show:

- Jurisdiction.
- Tax year.
- Rule-pack version.
- Current/candidate/confirmed status.
- Supporting source.
- Missing facts.
